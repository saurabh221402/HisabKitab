## F. Exceptional cases

Provide at least one real example for each that occurs:

- deal cancelled before delivery;
- partial delivery or several trucks;
- rate revised after quality/weight result;
- buyer rejects or returns goods;
- transit shortage/damage charged to transporter;
- seller paid in advance;
- buyer pays in advance;
- one payment covers several bills;
- payment does not match any bill;
- discount/write-off/disputed balance;
- party acts as both seller and buyer;
- direct seller-to-buyer movement without entering warehouse;
- own stock mixed across lots;
- correction after month or financial year closing.

For each case record: what happened, which paper records changed, exact calculation, who approved it, and the final effect on stock, party balance, cash/bank, and profit.
